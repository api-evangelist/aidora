## What Aidora did

Aidora automated the regulated leave-of-absence lifecycle for HR teams: eligibility determination
across federal FMLA and state paid-family-and-medical-leave programs, compliance forms and
documentation, return-to-work planning, and payroll coordination. Employees interacted with it
through a natural-language model by voice or text. Founded 2023 in San Francisco by Rhiana
Gademsky and Anant Agarwal; approximately $2.7M in seed funding from Team Ignite Ventures and
Fractal Software.

## Compliance

Aidora claimed SOC 2 Type II and used Vanta for continuous compliance monitoring. The claim was
published on its own /security-standards page, which no longer exists; it is recorded here from the
2026-04-15 archive snapshot and is not independently confirmed. Aidora explicitly analysed HIPAA
and documented that it does not apply, because PHI excludes general HR data and employer-held FMLA
medical information. Details: security/aidora-trust-center.yml.
